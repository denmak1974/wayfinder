# Wayfinder Roadmap

> Building independence one successful day at a time.

This roadmap describes the intended direction of the Wayfinder project.

Wayfinder is a Personal Independence Operating System for the Neurodiverse.

The purpose of Wayfinder is to help neurodiverse individuals build greater independence, confidence, executive functioning, life skills, and self-determination through practical and accessible technology.

The roadmap will evolve as we learn from users, caregivers, educators, therapists, researchers, and contributors.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every feature, module, and design decision should answer a simple question:

> Does this help a neurodiverse person become more independent?

If not, it should be reconsidered.

# Phase 1: Daily Readiness Assistant

Status: Planned

Goal:

> Help a neurodiverse adult successfully start each day.

Many daily challenges begin with simple decisions:

- What should I wear?
- What activities do I have today?
- What should I bring?
- What should I expect?

The Daily Readiness Assistant aims to reduce uncertainty and support successful daily routines.

## Planned Capabilities

### Weather Awareness

- Current weather conditions
- Daily forecast

### Schedule Awareness

- Calendar integration
