# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Wayfinder is a community dedicated to helping neurodiverse individuals build greater independence, confidence, and quality of life.

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and supportive environment for everyone, regardless of:

- Age
- Disability
- Neurotype
- Experience level
- Education
- Gender identity
- Nationality
- Race
- Religion
- Sexual orientation
- Socioeconomic background
- Technical ability

We believe the best solutions emerge when diverse perspectives are welcomed and respected.

---

## Our Guiding Principle

Wayfinder exists to support human dignity and independence.

As a community, we strive to treat one another with the same respect, patience, and empathy we hope our software will embody.

Before posting, commenting, submitting code, or participating in discussions, ask:

> Am I helping create a community where people feel safe, respected, and welcome?

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Listening to different perspectives
- Assuming positive intent
- Offering feedback kindly and professionally
- Being welcoming to newcomers
- Sharing knowledge openly
- Supporting accessibility and inclusion
- Focusing discussions on solving problems
- Respecting lived experiences and personal stories

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation
- Personal attacks or insults
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Hate speech
- Deliberate exclusion of others
- Trolling or inflammatory comments
- Unwelcome sexual language or advances
- Threatening behavior
- Sharing private information without permission
- Mocking or dismissing neurodiverse experiences
- Any behavior that makes another community member feel unsafe

---

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Accessibility is not optional within the Wayfinder community.

We recognize that contributors may have different communication styles, abilities, processing speeds, and support needs.

Please:

- Be patient
- Communicate clearly
- Avoid unnecessary jargon
- Respect different perspectives
- Allow room for questions and clarification

The goal is participation, not perfection.

---

## Disagreements

Disagreements will happen.

Healthy disagreement is often beneficial.

When disagreements occur:

- Focus on ideas, not people
- Assume good intent
- Support viewpoints with evidence where possible
- Remain respectful
- Be willing to compromise

Being passionate is welcome.

Being disrespectful is not.

---

## Project Mission Comes First

Wayfinder exists to help neurodiverse individuals become more independent.

When making decisions, discussing features, or resolving conflicts, we should return to the project's core question:

> Does this help a neurodiverse person become more independent?

Our shared mission should guide our actions.

---

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the project maintainers through GitHub's private reporting channel. Do not place personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

All reports will be reviewed thoughtfully and respectfully.

We are committed to maintaining a safe and inclusive environment for everyone.

Reports will be handled confidentially by maintainers without a conflict of interest. Enforcement decisions may be appealed to uninvolved maintainers.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take any action necessary to protect the health of the community, including:

- Providing guidance
- Issuing warnings
- Removing content
- Restricting participation
- Removing contributors from the project

Enforcement decisions will be made with the project's values of fairness, respect, and inclusion in mind.
