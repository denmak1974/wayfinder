# Vision

> Building independence one successful day at a time.

## Why Wayfinder Exists

Wayfinder began with a simple observation:

Many of the challenges people face each day are not large, dramatic problems.

They are small decisions.

- What should I wear today?
- What do I need to bring?
- Where do I need to go?
- What happens next?
- How do I begin?

For many people these questions are answered automatically.

For others, including many neurodiverse individuals, they may create friction, anxiety, uncertainty, and dependence.

Wayfinder exists to reduce that friction.

Not by making decisions for people.

Not by replacing caregivers.

Not by attempting to "fix" anyone.

Wayfinder exists to help people navigate everyday life with greater confidence, capability, and independence.

---

# Our Belief

We believe independence is not a destination.

It is a journey composed of thousands of small moments.

A successful morning.

A successful appointment.

A successful conversation.

A successful trip.

A successful day.

These moments accumulate into confidence.

Confidence becomes capability.

Capability becomes independence.

Technology should help create more of these moments.

---

# Our Vision

Wayfinder aspires to become a Personal Independence Operating System for the Neurodiverse.

An open platform that provides practical tools to support:

- Daily routines
- Executive functioning
- Learning
- Communication
- Daily living skills
- Community participation
- Employment readiness
- Self-advocacy
- Lifelong independence

Wayfinder is not a single application.

Wayfinder is a collection of capabilities working together toward a common goal:

> Helping individuals live with greater independence and self-determination.

---

# What We Mean by Independence

Independence does not mean doing everything alone.

Independence means having the ability, confidence, information, and support needed to make choices and participate fully in everyday life.

Different people will define success differently.

For one person, independence may mean selecting appropriate clothing for the day.

For another, it may mean taking public transit alone.

For another, it may mean managing a daily schedule.

Wayfinder should support each person's individual journey.

---

# The First Step

The first Wayfinder capability is intentionally simple.

## Daily Readiness Assistant

The Daily Readiness Assistant helps answer a few practical questions every morning:

- What is the weather?
- What activities do I have planned?
- What should I wear?
- What should I bring?
- What should I expect today?

These seemingly simple decisions often set the tone for an entire day.

Our first success is not building a platform.

Our first success is helping someone start their day more confidently than they could yesterday.

---

# Accessibility First

Accessibility is not a feature.

Accessibility is a foundational principle.

Wayfinder should be designed from the beginning to support:

- Autism spectrum needs
- ADHD
- Executive-function challenges
- Reading difficulties
- Cognitive disabilities
- Visual learners
- Diverse communication styles

The best user experience is the one people can actually use.

---

# Open Source First

Wayfinder is intended to be built openly.

We believe the most valuable ideas will come from people closest to the problem.

That includes:

- Neurodiverse individuals
- Families
- Caregivers
- Educators
- Therapists
- Researchers
- Accessibility specialists
- Software developers
- Designers

No single person, organization, or discipline has all the answers.

We want Wayfinder to become a community-driven effort that continuously learns and improves.

---

# Technology as a Tool

Wayfinder is not an AI project.

Wayfinder is not a cloud project.

Wayfinder is not a software project.

Technology is simply the mechanism through which we pursue the mission.

The mission remains constant:

> Help people become more independent.

As technology evolves, Wayfinder should evolve with it.

The mission should never change.

---

# What Wayfinder Is Not

Wayfinder is not:

- A surveillance system
- A behavior control system
- A replacement for caregivers
- A replacement for educators
- A replacement for therapists
- A generic chatbot

Wayfinder should empower.

Not monitor.

Support.

Not control.

Enable.

Not replace.

---

# Our North Star

Every discussion, design decision, contribution, and feature request should ultimately be measured against one question:

> Does this help a neurodiverse person become more independent?

If the answer is yes, we are moving in the right direction.

If the answer is no, we should reconsider.

---

# Looking Forward

We envision a future where Wayfinder supports individuals across many aspects of daily life.

A future where anyone can contribute new capabilities.

A future where families, caregivers, educators, and technologists collaborate openly.

A future where practical tools reduce barriers and expand possibilities.

Most importantly, we envision a future where more people can confidently say:

> "I can do this myself."

That future begins with one successful step.

Then another.

And another.

That is the future Wayfinder exists to help build.
