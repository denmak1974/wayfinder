# Governance

## Model

Project Wayfinder uses an open, merit-based governance model with protected authority for lived experience, accessibility, privacy, and security.

## Bodies

### Community Advisory Group

A compensated group of neurodiverse adults with varied support, communication, sensory, cultural, and technical experiences. It reviews product principles, core participant journeys, language, and major roadmap changes. Members may use accessible, private review formats.

### Maintainer Council

Responsible for releases, architecture coherence, contributor health, finances, and working-group coordination. Membership is earned through sustained, constructive contributions and confirmed by existing maintainers with advisory-group input.

### Working groups

Initially: Product and Research, Accessibility and Design, Mobile, Platform and Security, Localization and Content, Community and Governance.

Working groups publish charters, decisions, meeting notes, and current owners. They cannot override project-wide consent, accessibility, security, or independence principles.

## Decision process

1. Routine decisions use lazy consensus in the owning working group.
2. Cross-cutting or irreversible decisions use a public Request for Comment for at least 14 days.
3. Maintainers document the decision, evidence, objections, and review date.
4. The Community Advisory Group must approve material changes to core participant control, supporter powers, behavior-shaping mechanics, or product language.
5. Accessibility, privacy, or security maintainers may block a release on a substantiated critical risk.

When consensus fails, the Maintainer Council decides by a two-thirds vote and publishes the rationale. Conflicts of interest are disclosed and affected members abstain.

## Roles

| Role | Path | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contributor | Any accepted contribution | Participate and propose |
| Reviewer | Consistent domain contributions | Review within documented scope |
| Maintainer | Sustained review, delivery, and community stewardship | Merge and release within scope |
| Council member | Cross-project service and community trust | Project-wide decisions |
| Advisory member | Compensated lived-experience participation | Protected product-review authority |

Roles are reviewed annually and may be made emeritus after inactivity. Removal for conduct, security, or trust reasons follows a documented, private, appealable process.

## Independence test

Every roadmap proposal states:

- who becomes more independent;
- what real-world task improves;
- what evidence will demonstrate the change;
- what burden, risk, or supporter dependency it may introduce; and
- what simpler alternative was considered.

## Sustainability

- Publish annual financial and sponsor transparency reports.
- Do not accept sponsorship conditions that grant access to user data or roadmap control.
- Fund accessibility review, participant compensation, translation, moderation, and security before growth features.
- Maintain at least two release-capable maintainers and documented succession for critical areas.
- Seek neutral fiscal sponsorship once the contributor community requires shared funds.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes use a 30-day public Request for Comment, advisory-group review, and a two-thirds Maintainer Council vote.
